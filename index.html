Phone Product Timeline

Open interactive demo (generation comparison) →

Analysed smartphone price increases across product generations by consolidating launch dates, starting prices, and specifications from multiple brands and comparing models with equivalent specifications.

Problems solved

  • Competitor information is scattered across websites and reports, so every comparison means re-searching
  • Price changes across generations are hard to compare quickly
  • Colleagues' computers are locked down and can't install complex software

Approach & highlights

  • Price band × time matrix view; click a model for its full specs
  • Side-by-side spec comparison of any two phones, plus the starting-price change from the previous generation
  • Packaged with Python as a single .exe — double-click to run, no installation
  • Data is exchanged as standardized JS files, so non-engineering colleagues can update it too

Market Intelligence

Open interactive demo →

Consolidates the Excel reports of three third-party market research firms, unifies fields and definitions, then offers cross-filtering by brand, price band, and quarter with trend charts — replacing the manual quarterly reconciliation.

Problems solved

  • The three data sources differ in format, brand naming, and categories
  • Every quarterly update meant rebuilding a pivot analysis
  • Numbers that differ between sources were hard to view side by side

Approach & highlights

  • Normalizes multi-source Excel files into a single data format
  • Brand share, price-band structure, and quarterly trends on one page
  • Switch the data source to compare differences
  • Industry news ticker: Excel connects to RSS through a web link and Power Query reads it as a refreshable connection
  • An "Update news" button on the HTML page refreshes the news with one click each day once a folder is connected
  • Runs locally on 127.0.0.1, so data never leaves the computer

Work tool · Data tracking

Socket Tracker

Open interactive demo →

Tracks each model's sales period, chip, and supplier by brand and product line (Socket), showing who used which chip and when as month-by-month color blocks, with yearly and overall totals calculated automatically.

Problems solved

  • Product-line generation changes and chip-supplier switches were scattered across several sheets, making the timing hard to see at a glance
  • Every monthly update required confirming exactly what changed this time
  • Data had to move back and forth between Excel and the tool

Approach & highlights

  • Wide-table view merges color blocks by month automatically, with suppliers distinguished by color
  • Multiple views: long table, chip comparison, brand and supplier share
  • Shows added / deleted / modified differences before you confirm an update
  • Excel import and export, plus paste-from-Excel import

Personal project · Full-stack website

Industry Structure Watch

Open interactive demo (Chinese interface) →GitHub

A personal Taiwan-stock data site that looks at industry structure rather than stock tips: sector indices, institutional investor flows, ETF holdings, and monthly revenue rankings. Its tagline is Market Pulse, Not Advice.

Features

  • Panels for the Taiwan market, sector indices, and institutional net buying / selling
  • ETF holdings scraper (each fund company's site assessed one by one for scrapability)
  • Monthly revenue rankings and a watchlist
  • Google OAuth login with allowlist access control

Pitfalls along the way

  • Data errors caused by inconsistent field-name capitalization in the FinMind API
  • SSL certificate problems on the TPEx (Taipei Exchange) site
  • Moving sessions into MySQL to deploy on Cloud Run
  • Migrating from AWS EC2 to a self-hosted Ubuntu mini PC with Cloudflare Tunnel
